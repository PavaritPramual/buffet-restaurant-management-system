PATCH
/api/v1/orders/{id}/status
Advance an order to the next fulfillment status

KITCHEN_STAFF may advance RECEIVED to PREPARING and PREPARING to READY. SERVICE_STAFF may advance READY to SERVED. No other role may transition an order. Starting PREPARING consumes the recipe frozen at order placement atomically; insufficient or inactive stock returns 409 without changing order or stock.
